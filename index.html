Project · 2026

Energy data platform

How do wind and weather show up in Finland's day-ahead electricity price? I built an end-to-end platform on Azure and Databricks to find out: ingestion from three open APIs, lakehouse layers, dbt models and infrastructure as code.

Result

Wind explains up to two fifths of the price variation
