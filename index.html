Olen data engineer, jolla on tausta kauppatieteissä ja tulevaisuudentutkimuksessa. Yhdistän teknisen osaamisen ymmärrykseen siitä, mihin liiketoiminta dataa tarvitsee.

In English

I’m a data engineer with a background in business and strategic foresight. I work at Cloud2, where I build data platforms and pipelines in the cloud for enterprise clients. Before that, I built data pipelines and reporting at Skillio and developed strategic foresight at KONE.

My latest side project is an end-to-end energy data platform on Azure and Databricks, built with Terraform, dbt and GitHub Actions. It shows that wind explains up to two fifths of the variation in the Finnish day-ahead electricity price.
