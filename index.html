Projects

Data

  • Energy data platform 2026

    How do wind power and weather show up in the Finnish day-ahead electricity price? To find out, I built an end-to-end data platform on Azure and Databricks: ingestion from three open APIs, lakehouse layers, dbt models and infrastructure as code. Wind explains up to two fifths of the variation in the price. Built with Terraform, Azure, Databricks, dbt and GitHub Actions.

  • Betting odds and match results 2025

    How well do bookmakers’ odds match the actual results of football matches? An ETL practice project that fetches and cleans match data, loads it into PostgreSQL, moves it to BigQuery and reports on it in Power BI. Built with Python, PostgreSQL, BigQuery and Power BI.

  • Companion planting 2025

    An app that places the vegetables you pick on a garden grid using a simple scoring of which plants grow well together. Built with Python and Streamlit.

Research and foresight

  • Master’s thesis 2023

    Pioneering spirit and village community of the future: Images of the future of church members of Jätkäsaari in the year 2040. I ran a workshop for members of the Jätkäsaari parish where we sketched desirable futures for the district and the trends that support or threaten them.

  • Foresight projects at KONE 2022–2024

    Interviews and analysis for the Future of Living and Future of Mobility and Mobility Hubs projects, and a foresight playbook for the strategy and innovation teams that introduces the key methods of futures research from a business point of view.

  • Crown Bridges systems analysis

    A short study of how building the Crown Bridges tramway affects traffic flows in Helsinki, especially on the metro.

  • Bachelor’s thesis 2020

    Sustainability reports as a tool for customer dialogue: the case of Paulig. A content analysis of Paulig’s sustainability reports and their role in consumer communication.
